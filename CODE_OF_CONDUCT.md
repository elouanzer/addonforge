# Code of conduct

Addon Forge welcomes people of every background and level of experience, including those who have never written code. Many people here use it precisely because they aren't developers.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and with people writing in a language that isn't their first.
- Assume good faith, and explain rather than correct.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time and privacy: don't share someone's personal information, and don't ask anyone for theirs.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language, in any form.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Pressuring people to break the [content policy](CONTRIBUTING.md#content-policy) or a service's terms.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

Report a problem to the maintainers through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability** sends a private message), or by contacting [@elouanzer](https://github.com/elouanzer) on GitHub. Reports are handled privately. Depending on what happened, maintainers may edit or remove content, warn, or block contributors, temporarily or permanently.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
